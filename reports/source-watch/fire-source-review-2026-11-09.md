# Source review: warehouse fire report, 5 November 2026

**Record type:** source-attributed excerpt and audit note; not a raw official bulletin.  
**Review timestamp:** 2026-11-09.  
**Primary item referenced:** [Interfax-Ukraine, 00:05, 5 November 2026](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/1231365.html), citing Kyiv OVA head Tymur Tkachenko and linking the Telegram post [t.me/tkachenkotymur/3077](https://t.me/tkachenkotymur/3077).

Interfax attributed this short statement to Tkachenko:

> «У Бучанському районі внаслідок ворожої атаки сталося займання на території складу. Уточнюємо інформацію щодо постраждалих».

The article reported that local Telegram channels named Sofiivska Borshchahivka, while Interfax had no official confirmation of that location at publication time. This is an early attributed statement, not a complete capture of the original Telegram post and not a statement that no one was injured.

Later reporting on 5 November said that the warehouse guard had been injured and that the acting head of the village confirmed Sofiivska Borshchahivka as the location. See [Slovo i Dilo, 00:46](https://www.slovoidilo.ua/amp/2026/11/05/novyna/bezpeka/kyyivshhyni-pislya-ataky-zahorivsya-sklad-ye-postrazhdalyj) and [Forbes Ukraine, 5 November](https://forbes.ua/news/rosiia-atakuvala-skladsku-infrastrukturu-na-kiyivshchini-foto-05112026-43525). These are later secondary reports; they are not a substitute for a saved DSNS record or a direct capture of the original official posts.

## Provenance limitation

The retained file `data/processed/source_notes/kyiv_ova_fire_legacy_unverified.md` records the quarantine of a manually composed summary. It does not contain a captured original bulletin or the source text needed to verify its wording, and its sentence “Постраждалих немає” conflicts with later same-night reporting. The verification pipeline must not consume that file as a primary source. The original official Telegram post has not been archived in this repository, so the evidence passage below is attributed through Interfax and must be described as secondary reporting of an official statement.
