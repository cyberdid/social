## Reading the sequence

- The earliest located official written position is the Kyiv Region Police page at 17:27 on 2 Nov; MVS’s 22:30 statement later that evening says an internal review had already been ordered “during the day.” Neither supplies a complete event log.
- The 4 Nov MVS page explicitly refers to the situation as having occurred on 2 Nov. A supplementary machine-readable run keeps that as one unresolved, source-reported physical mention bounded to the local calendar day and stores the MVS page publication as a separate communication event ([event bundle](reported-event-reconstruction-2026-11-08/timeline.md)). This is not independent confirmation of event time.
- On 3 Nov, the Ombudsman described a request for scrutiny; DBR described an open investigation and questions to be checked; NGU/MVS spokespeople gave an account of the building and people inside; SBU announced investigative steps. These are different institutional roles and dated statements, not mutually corroborating witness records.
- The MVS/NGU briefing’s reported “26 inside” and the NGU-hosted/ArmyInform account of four injured servicemembers are not equivalent to a nominal roster or clinical record. The latter article itself leaves cause and responsibility to investigation. No medical record was captured.
- Later SBU/NPU counts have different publication dates and categories. They should not be combined into one count of suspects or treated as final findings.
- On 6 Nov, media reported custody decisions and courtroom statements. No primary court order was located in the captured materials; attribute details to the relevant outlet and speaker.
- The 08 Nov source-watch run is a capture date, not a new publication date. Its `new_page` label means first capture in that local archive root. Two DBR requests failed TLS, and the DBR video roundup was not substantively inspected.
