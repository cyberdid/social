## What can be completed autonomously, and what remains unproven

The software mechanics, adapters, regression fixtures, reports, privacy-safe receipts and additional deterministic tests can continue to be implemented and checked without outside experts. The evidence claims themselves cannot be marked validated merely by running more code: they require resolvable primary-source passages, independent source-root evidence, reference media/event facts, or a held-out adjudicated set. Where those inputs are absent, the valid output is `unknown`, `conflicting`, or `insufficient evidence`, not a forced verdict.

This status does not assert that the real incident lacked evidence or that no relevant media exists. It records only what the repository receipts currently demonstrate.

## Closeout rule

Do not mark the masterplan complete until the implemented pipeline has a reproducible real-source run; key claims link to locatable passages and preserve supporting/contradicting evidence; physical and communication timelines are separate; video claims are linked to inspectable media observations with validated time/place scope; the report passes its citation and semantic controls; and unresolved coverage gaps are explicit. If an evidence-dependent criterion remains unavailable, report that as an open validation limit rather than silently treating it as passed.
