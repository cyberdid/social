## Frame and allocation

The source contains 23,386 records. Recomputing the existing relevance decision yielded 1,438 selected records. After excluding 2,003 out-of-window records, the supplied in-window frame contains 21,383 records: 1,438 selected and 19,945 rejected records eligible for this false-negative audit.

We used stratified simple random sampling without replacement. Strata prioritize broad place/topic/reference candidates while retaining a random remainder. Allocation used proportional distribution with a minimum of two sampled records per non-empty stratum; ties are deterministic. The sample-size search uses a 95% normal approximation, worst-case binary variance 0.25, finite-population correction, and a target half-width of 0.05 for the weighted overall share within this finite frame.
