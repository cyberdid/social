# Measured author article typography

The five references were inspected as PDFs, including embedded font inventories and page-coordinate XML exported by Poppler. These are measured reference conventions, not a claim of compliance with every journal's submission rules.

| Element | Reference measurement | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | A4, 595.28 x 841.89 PDF points | article, a4paper |
| Body | CMUSerif-Roman, approx. 10.95 PDF pt | 11 TeX pt, bundled CMU Serif OTF |
| Body left edge | approx. 70.87 PDF pt | 25 mm margins |
| Body baseline spacing | approx. 13.55 PDF pt | standard article 11pt spacing |
| Title | regular CMU Serif, approx. 17.22 PDF pt | regular LARGE (17.28 TeX pt) |
| Section | CMU Serif Bold, approx. 14.35 PDF pt | standard article section |
| Subsection | CMU Serif Bold, approx. 11.96 PDF pt | standard article subsection |
| Author | regular CMU Serif, approx. 11.96 PDF pt | standard author size |
| Affiliations and abstract | CMU Serif, approx. 9.96 PDF pt | small |

| Figure text | DejaVu Sans in Matplotlib figures | Matplotlib bundled DejaVu Sans |
