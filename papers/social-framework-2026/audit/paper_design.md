# Paper design after technical audit

Subject: Social as an integrated, evidence-grounded automated analytical research framework. Not a second incident case study and not a new state-of-the-art algorithm.

RQ1: Which heterogeneous-source and typed-analysis components are actually integrated, and where are manual interfaces retained?
RQ2: Which provenance/lineage/locator invariants are enforced, and which evidentiary guarantees do they not establish?
RQ3: Do exact text-similarity and conditional randomization outputs reproduce under frozen inputs?
RQ4: How does the implementation separate communication time, supplied physical-event intervals and unknowns?
RQ5: What semantic/autonomy failure modes survive structural testing?
RQ6: What practical, bounded analyst workflow is supported without implying independent fact verification?

Contributions: audited integration map; concrete linked evidence model and bounded workflow; reproducible finite-corpus duplicate/statistical evaluation; explicit semantic counterexamples and autonomy boundary; privacy-conscious research artifact package. Original algorithm, universal reliability, independently validated physicaltruth and autonomous investigative completeness are not contributions.

Core manuscript will distinguish implementation/test/evaluation/validation. Six required tables and seven useful figures: actualarchitecture, evidencegraph, workflow/autonomy, dualtime, measuredcopy/nullresult, typedqualitymatrix, multimodalpipeline (qualitymatrix may remain table). Relatedsystems comparison qualitativeonly. Case application target15–20%maintext if feasible; empirical results elsewhere remain algorithm/systemtests with explicit case-conditioned scope.

Evidence hierarchy: fresh bounded computations > versioned execution artifacts > code properties > project report assertions > methodological plans. Primary literature contextualizes design, not evidence of Social implementation. Full offline tests are not independent semantic gold. Finalreview is separate AIcritique, not humanpeerreview.
